==============================================================================
                          YURI AI - OFFLINE ASSISTANT
==============================================================================

Yuri is a personal offline AI assistant inspired by JARVIS, powered by a
local Qwen neural core running via llama.cpp or Termux on Android and Windows.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANDROID INSTALLATION & USAGE:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Install `Yuri.apk` (or `YURI_AI_v1.0.apk`) on your Android device.
2. Grant Microphone and Camera permissions when prompted.
3. Features:
   - Voice Activation: Speak to Yuri or say "Yuri" to summon the floating orb.
   - Camera Vision Scanner: Tap the camera icon or say "Yuri, what is this?" /
     "Yuri, analyze this" to open the optical targeting HUD and scan real-world
     objects, circuits, or schematics.
   - Background Protocol: Displays the holographic floating bubble over any app.
   - Offline Qwen Engine: Connects to http://127.0.0.1:8080 (Termux llama-server).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WINDOWS USAGE:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Option A: Run `Yuri.exe` (Standalone 64-bit Windows PE executable)
- Launches the Yuri AI command console and connects to local Qwen.

Option B: Double-click `Yuri_Windows_Interactive.bat`
- Interactive Windows command console with speech synthesis and direct prompt loop.
